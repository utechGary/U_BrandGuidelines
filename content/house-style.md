# United Stables — House Style

Authoritative copy rules for all $U / United Stables deliverables. Titles use Title Case; body copy uses sentence case.

## Rule 1 — Title Case (titles / headlines only)
Capitalize major words (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns). Keep minor words lowercase UNLESS they are the first or last word of the title (first and last word are ALWAYS capitalized).

Minor words (lowercase mid-title):
- Articles: a, an, the
- Short prepositions (under 5 letters): at, by, for, in, on, to, up, with, as, of, off, out, via
- Coordinating conjunctions: and, as, but, for, if, or, nor, yet, so

## Rule 2 — No trailing punctuation on titles
Strip any trailing period, comma, or semicolon from titles. Question marks and exclamation marks may stay if intentional to the tone.

## Rule 3 — Body copy (sentence case)
Do not apply Title Case to paragraphs, card labels, stat callouts, bullets, captions. Normal sentence case: first word + proper nouns / brand names capitalized. Check subject-verb agreement, typos, punctuation, number/unit consistency, terminology consistency. Prefer the smallest edit that fixes the issue — do not rephrase wholesale (select-over-rewrite).

## Preserve exactly (never re-case)
Brand / product names & tickers: $U, United Stables, Binance, Binance Wallet, Bitget, BNB Chain, Ceffu, Mirror X, OpenEden, Aster, Aster DEX, Lista DAO, BSC, KuCoin, LBank, ListaDAO.
All-caps acronyms: AI, CEX, DEX, APR, LP, RWA.
Numbers / tickers / symbols: 100,000U, BTC/U, 1.91% — never touch.

## $U & amount formatting
- Ticker is `$U` in marketing copy; `United Stables` for legal / About only.
- Amount format: `100,000 $U` — no leading "$" before the number when the ticker follows.
- Compound modifiers hyphenated: on-chain, cross-border, near-real-time.
- American English spelling throughout.
