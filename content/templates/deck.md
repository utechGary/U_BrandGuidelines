# Template — On-brand deck (pptx)

Fonts: headings/mastheads = Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro (brand face); body = General Sans (document face, 200–700 + italics).

## Deck visual language
- Masthead: black logo top-left; gold bold-italic confidential tag top-right + gray subline; page number bottom-right.
- Stat callouts: huge display-face numbers alternating gold (#A18B2F) ↔ olive/moss (#6A7B4A); plain label below.
- Charts: sequential ramp black → cream → olive → gold (gold = peak/emphasis).
- Eyebrows: UPPERCASE, gold-muted (#7A6923) or moss green, with a short gold dash/rule.

## Slide types & token mapping
- Title slide: bg cream #DFDBCB or white; title Title Case (no trailing punctuation), gold accent on last word where the tagline is used.
- Section divider: dark green #223322 bg, gold #A18B2F number/eyebrow, inverse text #EAECEF.
- Content slide: white bg, ink #000000 headings, secondary #484848 body, gold for emphasis only.
- Stat / callout: gold ↔ olive alternating numbers; amount format `100,000 $U`.
- AI-Ready slide: EXCEPTION — dark treatment even if neighbours are light.

## Rules
Color priority: primary first, purple least. Titles Title Case; body sentence case. Preserve brand names exactly. Positioning = unified liquidity layer, not a coin. Logo colorways (Gold/Silver/Black/Dark Green/Dark Grey) — never recolour. No emoji.
