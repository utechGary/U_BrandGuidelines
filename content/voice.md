# United Stables — Voice & Positioning

## Core positioning
$U is a **unified liquidity layer** — the tagline is **"A United Layer of Money."**
Position it as infrastructure / a liquidity layer, never as a coin or token object.
Prefer "unified liquidity", "settlement", "infrastructure", "conduit" over "coin".

## Tagline
**United Layer of Money** — the last word ("Money") is always rendered in gold.

## Pillars
Fluid · United · Inclusive. (Plus AI-Ready, which always takes a distinct dark visual treatment.)

## Voice
Confident, institutional-fintech, optimistic. Specifics:
- Title Case headlines; UPPERCASE eyebrows.
- Short declarative sentences.
- Lists of three.
- No emoji, ever.

## Messaging emphasis
- Value flows freely between chains, platforms, and ecosystems — every $U backed 1:1 by institutional-grade reserves with unified liquidity.
- Stability, inclusivity, cross-ecosystem utility; institutional-grade yet globally inclusive.
