# United Stables — Voice & Positioning

## Core positioning
$U is a **settlement layer and infrastructure product**, not a typical crypto token. Every line of copy and every visual should reinforce this. Prefer "settlement layer", "unified liquidity", "infrastructure", "conduit" over "coin" or "token object".

## Pillars
Fluid · United · Inclusive. (Plus AI-Ready, which always takes a distinct dark visual treatment.)

## Messaging emphasis
- Stability, inclusivity, cross-ecosystem utility.
- Institutional-grade + globally inclusive ("from Nairobi to Silicon Valley").
- 1:1 backed, fully liquid, stablecoin-inclusive reserve, partner & user empowering.

## Tone
Confident, clean, editorial. Warm rather than cold dev-tool. Infrastructure-serious, not hype-crypto. Concise, declarative subtitles under short titles.

## Copy handling
When source copy is provided, SELECT / FILTER / ARRANGE — do not creatively rewrite unless explicitly asked.
